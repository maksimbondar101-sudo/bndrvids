About

Every listing already contains a video. Almost nobody makes one.

There are twelve photos sitting on a listing right now. Somebody paid for them, uploaded them, and then let them sit there as a grid of thumbnails nobody scrolls.

The observation

Video wins the click. Everyone knows this. Almost nobody does it.

Not because property managers don't believe it — but because getting video made means a vendor, a calendar, an access window, a unit that has to be empty and clean at a specific hour, and a week of waiting.

So the video only gets made for the flagship property, or the luxury lease-up, or not at all. The other ninety percent of the portfolio stays a grid of thumbnails.

BNDRVIDS exists because that last constraint was the only real one — and it isn't a constraint anymore.

Who you're dealing with

Maksim Bondar

I kept noticing the same thing on listing after listing: a dozen photographs somebody paid a photographer to take, sitting in a grid nobody scrolls past. The video that would actually move the unit never got made — not because anyone doubted video works, but because making one meant booking a vendor, holding an access window, and having the place empty and clean at a specific hour.

The one rule I don't bend: the video only shows what your photos show. Nothing borrowed from another property, nothing from a stock library, no rooms that don't exist. People commit to places off these videos without ever standing in them. It has to hold up the day they walk through the door.

Where the line is

Four things we won't do, so you don't have to ask.

Invent space

If a room isn't in your photos, it isn't in the video. No exceptions, and no filling a gap with something that photographs close enough.

Sell you drone or twilight

We work from what you photographed. If nobody flew a drone over that building, we can't put one in a video and won't charge you for pretending.

Lock you into a contract

No term, no retainer, no minimum. One video for one unit is a complete transaction. Nothing to cancel, because there's nothing to sign.

Charge for work you don't like

You watch the whole thing on the call before anything is invoiced. If it misses, you don't pay — we'd sooner eat the work than have it out on your listings with our hands on it.
