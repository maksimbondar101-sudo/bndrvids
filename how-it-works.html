How it works

There is no process. That's the product.

Property video normally means a booking, an access window, a clean unit, a crew, and a week. We removed all five. What's left takes you about ten seconds.

The four steps

01

Send the listing link

Zillow, Apartments.com, Rent.com, your own site, an MLS sheet, or a folder of raw photos — whatever you already have. If the photos are public, that's all we need. If they aren't, drop them in a folder and send that instead.

02

We build the walkthrough

We pull the photo set, order the rooms the way a visitor would actually move through the property, hold on the details that sell it, and cut the vertical versions for social at the same time.

03

We walk you through it on a call

We play the finished walkthrough, show you which photos became which shots, and answer whatever you want to ask. Small changes we handle on the call — swapping the music, reordering a room, correcting a rent figure on screen. Twenty minutes. You confirm it there and then, or you don't: if it isn't for you, that's the end of it and there's no follow-up sequence.

04

Approve, pay, and it's yours

Confirm on the call and we email an invoice straight after. Once payment clears, the finished files land in your inbox — master, portal-safe exports and the vertical versions. Nothing is charged before you've watched the video.

Built from listing photos

We never show a room that isn't in your photos.

Buyers and renters commit off video without ever standing in the property. That makes an invented hallway, or a room that photographs differently than it exists, your problem — not just ours.

So the rule is simple and we don't bend it: the walkthrough covers what your photo set covers. Nothing is substituted from another property, nothing is borrowed from a stock library, and no space is shown that you didn't photograph.

If the photos don't cover the second bedroom, the video doesn't either — and we'll tell you that before we start rather than fill the gap.
